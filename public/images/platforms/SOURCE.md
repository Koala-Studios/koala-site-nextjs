# Platform marks

Official brand glyphs from Simple Icons 16.37.0 (npm `simple-icons`, CC0 1.0), copied unchanged:
shopify, meta, instagram, facebook, google, googleads, tiktok, googlemaps.

They name the platforms we work in. They are not partner, certification or endorsement badges.
Each brand's own usage guidelines still apply (Google asks for its marks in full colour in some contexts).
